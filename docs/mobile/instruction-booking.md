# Book a Flight Lesson (Mobile)

Use the instruction booking flow to find an available instructor, choose a lesson type, and confirm the session — all from your phone.

## What you'll need

- Active club selected
- Approved membership
- At least one certified instructor available in your club

## Where to start

You can start an instruction booking from two places:

**From the calendar (reservation-anchored)**
1. Open the reservation calendar and tap an existing aircraft reservation.
2. Tap **Book Instruction** (or **Add Instruction**) on the reservation detail.
3. The booking wizard opens pre-filled with your reservation's time window.

**From your profile (standalone)**
1. Open **Pilot**.
2. Tap **Reserve Instructor**.
3. The booking wizard opens with no pre-filled time — you choose the window.

*Screenshot: [Pilot profile with Reserve Instructor menu item]*

## Step 1 — Pick your lesson window and type

1. Set the **date**, **start time**, and **end time** for the lesson (30-minute increments).
2. Select a **lesson type** (e.g. dual, solo prep, ground, check ride prep).
3. Tap **See Available Instructors**.

If you arrived from a reservation, the time window is already filled in from that reservation. You can still adjust it.

:::tip
If no instructors appear, try a different date or time. Instructor availability windows and blocks are set by each instructor.
:::

## Step 2 — Choose an instructor

The app shows instructors who are free for your requested window. Each card shows:

- Instructor name and rating
- Hourly instruction rate (if set)
- Available time slots for your window

Tap an instructor card to select them. A time slot highlights when chosen.

*Screenshot: [Instructor list with available time slots]*

## Step 3 — Confirm the lesson

Review the full lesson summary:

- Instructor and lesson type
- Scheduled date and time
- Estimated instruction cost (if rate is set)

If the booking is linked to an existing reservation, a note confirms the aircraft linkage.

Tap **Confirm** to book. Your instructor is notified automatically.

A success banner confirms the lesson was created.

## View and manage your lessons

After booking, your lessons appear in the instructor calendar:

1. Open **Pilot**.
2. Tap **Teaching Schedule** (if you are an instructor viewing your own bookings) — or access upcoming lessons from the reservation detail for student-booked lessons.

## Reschedule a lesson

1. Open the lesson from your reservation detail or instructor calendar.
2. Tap **Reschedule**.
3. Pick a new date and time slot.
4. Confirm the change.

The instructor is notified of the new time.

## Cancel a lesson

1. Open the lesson detail.
2. Tap **Cancel Lesson**.
3. Confirm.

Cancellation policies are set by your club admin.

## Common issues

**"No instructors available for this time"**
- Try adjusting the start or end time by 30 minutes.
- Instructor availability is set per instructor — not all instructors are available all day.

**"Add Instruction button is not visible"**
- You must own the aircraft reservation. You cannot add instruction to another pilot's reservation.
- Instruction add-on is only available within the allowed booking window set by your admin.

**Reservation-anchored vs. standalone lessons**
- Reservation-anchored: instruction is linked to an existing aircraft block. Aircraft booking and instruction are tracked together.
- Standalone: instruction is booked first; aircraft booking is handled separately if needed.
