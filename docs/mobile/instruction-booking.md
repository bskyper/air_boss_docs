# Book a Flight Lesson (Mobile)

Use the instruction booking flow to find an available instructor, choose a lesson time, and confirm the session — all from your phone. After booking you can track and manage lessons from the **Instruction** section of your profile.

## What you'll need

- Active club selected
- Approved membership
- At least one certified instructor available in your club (set by your admin)

## Where to start

Open **Pilot**, scroll to the **Instruction** section, then choose what you need:

| Option | Who sees it | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Reserve Instructor** | All members | Opens the booking wizard |
| **My Lessons** | All members | View and manage your booked lessons |
| **Teaching Schedule** | Instructors only | View lessons you are scheduled to teach |
| **My Availability** | Instructors only | Set your weekly and one-off availability |

---

## Booking a lesson

### Step 1 — Pick your lesson window

1. Tap **Reserve Instructor** from the Instruction section.
2. Set the **date**, **start time**, and **end time** for the lesson (30-minute increments).
3. Tap **See Available Instructors**.

:::tip
If you started from an existing aircraft reservation, the time window is pre-filled. You can still adjust it.
:::

### Step 2 — Choose an instructor

The app shows instructors who are free for your requested window. Each card shows:

- Instructor name and rating
- Hourly instruction rate (if set)
- Available time slots within your window

Tap an instructor card to select them.

:::tip
If no instructors appear, try a different date or time. Availability is set per instructor.
:::

### Step 3 — Confirm the lesson

Review the full lesson summary:

- Instructor and lesson type
- Scheduled date and time
- Estimated instruction cost (if rate is set)

Tap **Confirm** to book. Your instructor receives an automatic notification.

A success banner confirms the lesson was created.

## View and manage your lessons (students)

1. Open **Pilot → My Lessons**.
2. Use the **Upcoming** and **Past** tabs to find lessons.
3. Tap a lesson to view details or cancel it.

Cancellation policies are set by your club admin.

---

## Instructor: Manage your teaching schedule

### View upcoming lessons

1. Open **Pilot → Teaching Schedule**.
2. Switch between **Upcoming** and **Past** tabs.
3. Tap a lesson to:
   - **Complete** — mark a finished lesson and add notes
   - **Cancel** — cancel the lesson (student is notified)
   - **Edit notes** — update lesson notes at any time

### Set your availability

Your availability controls which time windows students can book with you.

1. Open **Pilot → My Availability**.
2. Configure:
   - **Weekly schedule** — recurring day/time windows (e.g. "Every Tuesday 9 AM–12 PM")
   - **One-off slots** — single-date availability additions
   - **Date blocks** — specific dates you are unavailable
3. Set your **hourly instruction rate** inline on the same screen.

Students can only book lessons during windows you mark as available (or outside of any blocks, if you have no recurring schedule set).

## Common issues

**"No instructors available for this time"**
- Try adjusting the start or end time by 30 minutes.
- Instructor availability is set per instructor — not all instructors are available all day.

**"Add Instruction button is not visible" (on a reservation)**
- You must own the aircraft reservation. You cannot add instruction to another pilot's reservation.
- Instruction add-on is only available within the booking window set by your admin.

**Reservation-anchored vs. standalone lessons**
- **Reservation-anchored:** instruction is linked to an existing aircraft block. Aircraft booking and instruction are tracked together.
- **Standalone:** instruction is booked first; aircraft booking is handled separately if needed.
