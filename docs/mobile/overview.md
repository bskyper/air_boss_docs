# Mobile App Overview

Air Boss mobile is built for day-of-flight use: reserving aircraft, logging flights, reporting issues, and managing your profile.

Recent app updates also reduce startup time, so the app should open and reach the main workflow faster.

## Core areas

- **Tower:** club snapshot and operational overview
- **Comms:** activity feed and club broadcasts
- **Fleet:** aircraft list, reserve flow, and aircraft operations
- **Hangar:** club details, member/admin tools, and reports
- **Pilot:** profile, documents, and app/security settings

## Typical pilot workflow

1. Open **Fleet** and choose an aircraft.
2. Tap **Reserve** to book time.
3. Start and end your flight in the app.
4. Log squawks if needed.
5. Keep profile and documents up to date under **Pilot**.
