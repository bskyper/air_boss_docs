# Create Your Air Boss Account

Use this guide if you are brand new to Air Boss.

## What you'll need

- An email address you can access
- A password (at least 8 characters)
- Your full name

That's it. A profile photo is optional, and you can add one later.

:::info
Air Boss no longer asks for a phone number or emergency contact at sign-up. You are asked for those later, only when they are first needed — see [Details Air Boss asks for later](#details-air-boss-asks-for-later).
:::

## Sign up on mobile (iOS/Android)

1. Open the app and tap **Sign Up**.
2. Enter:
   - Email
   - Password (at least 8 characters)
   - Confirm password
3. Tap **Create Account**. A short two-step wizard follows.
4. **Step 1 — Account Details:**
   - Full name
   - Optional profile photo
5. **Step 2 — Join a Club:** answer **"Do you have a club key to join a flying club?"**
   - **Yes**: enter your **Club Key** and **Club Password**, plus an emergency contact name and phone under **Before you fly** (clubs need someone to call in an emergency).
   - **No**: continue without a club. You can join or create one any time later.
6. Submit.

*Screenshot: [Mobile sign-up and two-step wizard]*

:::warning
Mobile sign-up does not have club search by name. You must use the exact club key and club password from your club admin.
:::

## Sign up on web

1. Go to [www.air-boss.club](https://www.air-boss.club) and open **Sign up**.
2. Enter your account details:
   - Email
   - Full name
   - Password (at least 8 characters)
   - Confirm password
   - Optional profile photo
3. Submit sign-up.

*Screenshot: [Web sign-up form with the four account fields]*

## Confirm your email

After sign-up, Air Boss sends a confirmation email. Click the link in that email to activate your account.

- Didn't get it? Check spam, then use **Resend confirmation email**. The resend becomes available again after a 30-second cooldown.
- If you sign up with an email that already has an account, you'll see **"An account with this email already exists. Please sign in instead."** — use the sign-in page (or password reset) instead.

### Where you land after confirming (web)

The web app returns you to whatever you were doing when you signed up:

- Signed up from the instructor network (a directory card or an instructor's public profile)? You land back in the network, connected to where you left off.
- Signed up from the **For Instructors** page? You land on the instructor profile editor to build your listing.
- Signed up from a **Join your club** or **Start a club** button? You land on the join-club or create-club page.
- Otherwise you land in the pilot workspace.

## Details Air Boss asks for later

Instead of collecting everything up front, Air Boss asks for contact details at the moment they are first needed:

- **Phone number** — asked the first time you send an instructor connection request, so your instructor can reach you. US 10-digit format.
- **Emergency contact (name and phone)** — asked at your first flying-adjacent step, under the heading **Before you fly**: joining or creating a club, or sending your first lesson-time request to an instructor. Clubs and instructors need someone to call in an emergency.

Each is asked only once, and you can also add or edit both any time from your profile settings.

## After sign-up

- If your club requires manual approval, you are sent to a **Pending Approval** screen.
