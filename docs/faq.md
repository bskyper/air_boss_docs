# FAQ

## Account and Access

### How do I create an account?
Use [Sign Up](/getting-started/signup). On both mobile and web, sign-up asks only for your email, a password (at least 8 characters), and your full name — a profile photo is optional. After sign-up, click the link in the confirmation email to activate your account.

### Why wasn't I asked for a phone number or emergency contact at sign-up?
Air Boss asks for those when they are first needed instead: your phone number the first time you send an instructor connection request, and your emergency contact the first time you join or create a club or request a lesson time (the **Before you fly** step). You can also add both any time in your profile settings.

### I got "An account with this email already exists" — what now?
That email is already registered. Use the sign-in page instead, or reset your password if you've forgotten it.

### How do I join a club?
Use your club's **club key** and **club password**. Air Boss does not use club-name search for joining.

### Why am I on "Pending Approval"?
Your membership is waiting for admin approval. This is normal for clubs using approval workflow.

### Can I join multiple clubs?
Yes. Membership is club-scoped. Choose the active club before booking or uploading documents.

## Password and Login

### What are the password requirements?
At least 8 characters, on both mobile and web. Passwords that have appeared in a known data breach are also rejected — if you see that message, choose a different password.

### How do I change my password on mobile?
Go to **Pilot > Edit Profile > Change Password**.

### How do I reset my password on web?
Use the password reset action on **Profile** to send a reset email.

### Can I use biometric login on mobile?
Yes, if your device supports it. Enable in **Pilot > Biometric Settings**, then use the biometric button on login.

## Documents

### Why is my document still pending?
Document review is done by club admins. You may see pending until they approve or request changes.

### What file types are accepted on web documents?
`PDF`, `PNG`, `JPG`, `JPEG`, and `HEIC` (max 10 MB per file).

### Why do required documents change?
Requirements are defined per club. Switching active club can change required document lists.

## Scheduling and Flights

### Where do I book on mobile?
Go to **Fleet**, select an aircraft, then tap **Reserve**.

### Why did my booking fail?
Common reasons:

- Membership is pending/inactive
- Time is invalid (30-minute increments)
- Conflict with another reservation
- Maintenance overlap or hold

### Can I edit or cancel a reservation?
Yes, you can edit/cancel your own reservations from schedule views.

### Where do I log flights on web?
Use **My Flights** (`/app/flights`). Use **All Flights** for club-wide review.

## Squawks and Maintenance

### What is a squawk?
A squawk is an issue report for an aircraft (mechanical, avionics, etc.).

### Where can I track squawk progress?
- Pilot view: **Squawks** in `/app/squawks`
- Admin view: maintenance board in `/dashboard/maintenance`

## Notifications and Theme

### Where do I change notification settings?
- Mobile: **Pilot > Notification Preferences**
- Web: `/app/settings/notifications`

### Can I change app theme on mobile?
Yes. Go to **Pilot > Theme Settings** and choose Light, Dark, or System.

## Billing

### What is billing hold?
Billing hold means a club trial/subscription is not active. Access to scheduling/aircraft/maintenance can be paused until billing is resolved.

### Who can fix billing hold?
Club admins can update billing from `/dashboard/billing`.

## Troubleshooting

### The app seems out of sync
1. Confirm your active club.
2. Refresh the page/feed.
3. Sign out and back in if needed.

### I found a bug
Use **Send Feedback** in the mobile app profile area and include:

- What you were doing
- What happened
- Tail number/club context (if relevant)
- Screenshot
