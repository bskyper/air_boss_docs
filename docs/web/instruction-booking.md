# Book a Flight Lesson (Web)

Use **Book Instruction** in the pilot workspace to schedule a lesson with a certified instructor.

## What you'll need

- Active club selected
- Approved membership
- At least one instructor available in your club

## Open the booking flow

**Standalone booking:**
1. In the pilot workspace, open **Book Instruction** from the navigation.

**From an existing reservation:**
1. Open **Schedule**.
2. Click your reservation block.
3. Click **Book Instruction** in the reservation preview.

When launched from a reservation, the wizard is pre-filled with that reservation's aircraft and time window.

## Step 1 — Pick date and time

1. Set the **date** using the date picker.
2. Set **start time** and **end time** (30-minute increments, 6:00 AM – 9:00 PM).
3. Click **Find Instructors**.

*Screenshot: [Book Instruction form with date, start time, end time fields]*

## Step 2 — Choose a combo

The app returns available instructor + aircraft combinations for your time window. Each combo card shows:

- Instructor name and rate
- Aircraft tail number, make, and model
- Estimated instruction cost and aircraft cost for the session

Click a combo card to select it, then click **Review Booking**.

:::tip
If the list is empty, try a wider time window or a different date. Availability is driven by each instructor's schedule.
:::

## Step 3 — Confirm the booking

Review the full lesson summary:

- Instructor and aircraft
- Scheduled date and time window
- Estimated hours and costs

Click **Confirm** to create the booking. An aircraft reservation is created automatically if one does not already exist for the selected combo.

A success confirmation appears when the lesson is saved.

## Manage booked lessons

Booked instruction sessions appear on the **Schedule** page as linked blocks and in your **Manage Reservations** panel alongside your aircraft reservations.

## Common issues

**No combos appear after step 1**
- Instructor availability windows may not cover your requested time. Try adjusting start or end time by 30 minutes.
- Confirm an instructor has been assigned to your club by an admin.

**"Booking window is locked" message**
- Some clubs restrict how far in advance instruction can be added to a reservation. Contact your admin if you need to book outside the standard window.

**Combo shows an aircraft you did not request**
- The combo finder matches instructors to aircraft they are rated for within your club. The aircraft shown is the best available match for the instructor and time.
