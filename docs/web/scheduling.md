# Book Aircraft on the Web

Use **Schedule** in the pilot workspace to create, edit, and cancel reservations.

## What you'll need

- Active club selected
- Approved membership
- Aircraft access granted by an admin

## Navigate the schedule

1. Open **Schedule**.
2. Choose **Week** or **Month** view with the toggle at the top right.
3. Move between dates with **Previous / Today / Next**.
4. Click **Refresh** to pull in reservations made by other pilots since the page loaded.

*Screenshot: [Schedule page header with Week/Month toggle and navigation buttons]*

:::tip
Week view shows a 5-day timeline — best for spotting conflicts before booking. Month view shows the full month — best for planning ahead.
:::

## Read the aircraft legend

Below the header, the legend lists each tail number, its color block, and the number of reservations visible in the current view.

- Click a tail number to hide or show that aircraft's blocks on the timeline.
- All aircraft are shown by default.
- Aircraft counts update as you move between weeks or months.

*Screenshot: [Aircraft legend with color blocks, tail numbers, and reservation counts]*

## Read a reservation block

Reservation blocks appear on the timeline in the aircraft's assigned color.

- **Confirmed** reservations show in a solid color.
- **Pending** reservations show in amber.
- **Maintenance** blocks show in a muted style.

Click any block to open a preview with the pilot name, aircraft, and time range. You cannot edit another pilot's reservation from the pilot workspace.

## Create a reservation

1. In the **New Reservation** form on the left, select an **Aircraft**.
2. Set the **Start date** and **Start time** (30-minute intervals).
3. Set the **End date** and **End time** (30-minute intervals).
4. Enter a **Purpose** (optional).
5. Click **Save**.

Your reservation block appears on the schedule immediately.

*Screenshot: [New Reservation form with aircraft selector, date/time pickers, and purpose field]*

:::tip
Past times are filtered out for the current day, so only valid future start times appear in the dropdown.
:::

## Handle a maintenance overlap warning

If your booking overlaps a scheduled maintenance block, Air Boss shows a warning before saving.

1. Read the warning details.
2. Check the acknowledgment box to confirm you understand the overlap.
3. Click **Save** again.

The reservation is saved alongside the maintenance block. Coordinate with your admin if there is a conflict.

## Manage your reservations

Open the **Manage Reservations** panel (below the form) to see all your upcoming reservations in a list.

Each row shows the aircraft, start time, and end time. From this panel you can:

- Click **Edit** to modify the aircraft, times, or purpose.
- Click **Cancel** to remove the reservation.

:::warning
Only your own future reservations are editable. Past reservations and other pilots' reservations are read-only from the pilot workspace.
:::

## Edit a reservation

1. Click your reservation block on the timeline **or** click **Edit** in the Manage Reservations panel.
2. Update the aircraft, dates, times, or purpose.
3. Click **Save**.

The block updates on the schedule immediately.

## Cancel a reservation

1. Open **Manage Reservations**.
2. Find the reservation you want to remove.
3. Click **Cancel**.

The block is removed from the schedule immediately.

## Common issues

**"Booking disabled" message instead of the form**
Your membership is pending approval or aircraft access has not been granted. Contact your club admin.

**Reservation is not visible after saving**
Click **Refresh**. The schedule syncs in real time, but a manual refresh resolves stale state.

**Maintenance block is covering your preferred time**
You can book over a maintenance block with acknowledgment, but confirm with your admin that the aircraft will be available.
