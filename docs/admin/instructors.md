# Manage Instructors

Use `/dashboard/instructors` to designate members as instructors, manage their rates, and monitor lessons across the club.

## Enabling the Instructors feature

The Instruction feature must be turned on for your club before instructor management and booking flows appear.

1. Open **Dashboard → Club Settings → General**.
2. Enable the **Instruction** toggle.
3. Save changes.

Once enabled, the **Instructors** entry appears in the admin navigation, and the instruction booking flow becomes available to members on web and mobile.

## Designating a member as an instructor

Only admins can grant or revoke instructor status.

1. Open **Dashboard → Instructors**.
2. Locate the member in the instructor table.
3. Toggle the **Instructor** switch on their row.

The member's profile is updated immediately. They gain access to:
- **My Availability** (set weekly schedule, one-off slots, date blocks)
- **Teaching Schedule** (view and manage booked lessons)

Toggling the switch off removes instructor access and hides those surfaces.

:::tip
If a member does not appear in the Instructors table, check that they are an approved member in **Dashboard → Users**.
:::

## Instructor rates

Instructors can set their own hourly rate from their profile. Admins can view (but not edit) the rate from the Instructors panel.

The rate appears on booking combo cards shown to students during the lesson booking flow.

## Viewing lessons (admin)

1. Open **Dashboard → Lessons**.
2. View all scheduled, completed, and cancelled lessons across the club.
3. Admins can cancel any lesson from this view.

The Lessons panel shows instructor name, student name, aircraft, date/time, and lesson status.

## Instructor calendar

Each instructor's detailed schedule is visible from **Dashboard → Instructors → [instructor name]**:

- **Day view** — timeline with lesson blocks and a detail panel on click
- **Week view** — 7-column grid with event badges
- **Month view** — color-coded calendar (available: green, blocked: red, unset: grey) with booking dots overlay

This view is read-only for admins. Instructors can edit their own availability from their profile.
