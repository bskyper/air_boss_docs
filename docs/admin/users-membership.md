# Manage Members and Approvals

Use `/dashboard/users` and `/dashboard/users/pending` to control club access.

## Pending approvals

1. Open **Pending**.
2. Review member details.
3. Click **Approve** or **Deny**.

Approved members move into the main Members list.

*Screenshot: [Pending members table with Approve and Deny buttons]*

## Member controls in `/dashboard/users`

For each member, admins can:

- Change role (member/admin)
- Toggle active/inactive access
- Toggle maintenance-team assignment (if permitted)
- Toggle instructor status (see [Manage Instructors](/admin/instructors))
- Review contact and ratings
- Review document status summary
- Manage per-aircraft approvals

## Document visibility and workflow

Member rows show document rollups such as:

- Verified count
- Pending review
- Expired or expiring items

For deeper review actions and feedback workflows, use [Document Review](/admin/document-review).

## Aircraft approvals

Use aircraft approvals when your club requires checkout-specific authorization per tail.

:::tip
Keep role elevation limited. Use admin role only for members who actively manage operations.
:::
